// About

From hands-on engineering to platform architecture

I work across the full platform lifecycle — data engineering, AI/ML enablement, and the operational backbone (DataOps, MLOps, LLMOps, AIOps) that keeps enterprise platforms dependable in production. My focus is architecting intelligent data platforms on Databricks and Spark for global CPG and pharmaceutical clients, where data is high-volume, heavily regulated, and central to commercial decisions — and increasingly, putting agentic AI to work to drive self-healing, zero-touch operations.

What I do best is turn sprawling, hard-to-trust data estates into governed, observable, production-grade platforms that business teams actually rely on — pairing deep hands-on engineering with architectural judgment, and growing the engineers around me as the platforms scale.
